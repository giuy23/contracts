## Brand & Style
This design system marries the discipline of master Japanese comic illustration (mangaka craft, paneling mechanics, dynamic hatching) with high-end luxury editorial design and bushido ceremonial aesthetics. It evokes absolute mastery, disciplined storytelling, cinematic tension, and artisanal prestige. 

The target audience encompasses high-tier collectors, art directors, luxury graphic novel connoisseurs, and top-tier creative studios commissioning bespoke narrative worlds. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- Razor-sharp panel framing inspired by comic panels, katana sheaths, and tsuba sword-guard geometry.
- Tactile Washi paper backgrounds offset by dense Sumi calligraphic ink splashes, high-contrast dynamic action imagery, and cinnabar vermilion hanko seal accents.
- Structured editorial balance: stark negative space paired with micro-ornamental details like corner notches, fine hairline crosshairs, dot matrices, and diamond lozenge markers (`❖`, `◆`).

## Colors
The palette is rooted in classical Japanese printmaking and luxury calligraphy:
- **Primary (`#8F1519`):** Deep Lacquer Crimson / Vermilion Hanko Red. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key diamond markers, and high-impact structural borders.
- **Secondary (`#1B1917`):** Pure Sumi Ink. A deep, organic charcoal-black carrying subtle warm mineral undertones. Used for display typography, comic linework, and high-contrast frames.
- **Tertiary (`#C92A2A`):** Vivid Cinnabar. Used sparingly for hover glows, notification indicators, bloodline rules, and active micro-badges.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F2`):** Textured Washi Parchment. An organic, tactile paper tone that eliminates digital glare and establishes an archival, artisanal foundation.

### Supporting Chromatics & Tonal Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF7F2` (Light Washi) / `#0D0D11` (Optional Sumi Night mode).
- **Surface Elevation (Cards/Containers):** `#FFFFFF` with razor hairline boundaries in `#E3DDD3`.
- **Muted Text / Editorial Captions:** `#736B63` (Kogane Grey).
- **Hairline Borders:** `#DDD5C8` (Parchment Hairline) and `#8F1519` (Vermilion Accent Rule).

## Typography
The typographical hierarchy balances high-fashion Japanese editorial drama with surgical mechanical metadata:
- **Display & Headlines (Bodoni Moda):** Extreme stroke contrast, sharp serifs, and high vertical axis elegance echo traditional chisel carvings and high-end editorial book cover titling. Used for headers, hero announcements, and chapter divisions.
- **Body & Longform (Source Sans 3):** Clean, open, humanist grotesque sans providing effortless legibility against textured washi backgrounds without competing with dramatic manga illustrations.
- **Labels & Micro-Data (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced, highly tracked, uppercase layout tags (`{ DETAIL PENGERJAAN }`, `[ 01 CONCEPT ]`, `VOL.04`). Emphasizes structural discipline, technical draftsmanship, and script storyboard notation.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model operates as a calibrated editorial manga grid:
- **Columns & Grid System:** 12-column layout on desktop with asymmetric panel spreads (e.g., 7-column hero illustration intersecting a 5-column editorial text block). On mobile, panels stack vertically with crisp dividing borders.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Generous section gutters (`space-2xl`) emulate Japanese *Ma* (negative space, breathing room), framing intense, high-density art panels.
- **Panel Breakouts & Asymmetry:** Illustrations break out of standard bounds into circular sun discs (`ensō`) or clipped octagonal shields, bleeding across grid lines with sumi ink spatter fringes.
- **Connective Flow:** Step indicators and workflow panels connect horizontally through dashed vermilion guidelines and directional chevron carats (`→`).

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with classical woodblock prints and physical manga plates, elevation is created through planar framing, razor outlines, and graphic overlaps rather than diffuse blur shadows:
- **Surface Elevation:** Cards and panels sit flat upon the Washi canvas with a 1px crisp outline in `#DDD5C8`. On hover, the frame transitions to `#8F1519` with an inset corner accent.
- **Sumi Layering:** Visual depth is achieved through stark physical layering: background washi → ink dot matrix / circular crimson ensō → cut-out dynamic character artwork → floating hairline HUD panels and stamp seals.
- **Tactile Shadow:** When drop shadow is strictly necessary (e.g., floating modal or drawer), it uses a tight, hard-edged Japanese screen shadow: `4px 4px 0px rgba(27, 25, 23, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
The shape language is strictly unapologetic, razor-sharp, and architectural:
- **Zero Radii (`0px`):** True to manga panel borders and katana cuts, containers, cards, and input fields utilize pure rectangular geometries with sharp 90-degree corners.
- **Angled & Octagonal Cuts:** Featured panels and primary showcase items utilize 45-degree chamfered corners (katana tsuba cuts), creating distinct octagonal silhouetting.
- **Diamond Accents (`◆`):** Square diamond lozenges serve as structural fasteners at card corners, list bullet nodes, and section header dividers.
- **Framing Brackets:** Micro brackets (`[ ]` and `{ }`) wrap section labels, invoking editorial storyboards and drafting documentation.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Bars
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Cinnabar/Vermilion (`#8F1519`), pure rectangular cut with crisp 1px tone-on-tone inner border. Text is uppercase `Source Sans 3` semi-bold or `JetBrains Mono` with wide tracking, flanked by a trailing filled diamond or arrow (`+`, `→`, `❖`). On hover, background shifts to intense cinnabar (`#C92A2A`) with micro-translation.
- **Secondary / Outline CTA:** Transparent base, 1px Sumi Ink (`#1B1917`) or vermilion hairline border. Corner registration notches (1px outward crosses at corners). 
- **Floating Hanko Seal:** Circular or square red seal button featuring traditional stylized kanji monogram, functioning as quick-action trigger or authenticity stamp.

### Cards & Comic Panels
- **Comic Panel Card:** Crisp 1px border (`#DDD5C8`). Image sits flush at top or fully fills the frame with high-contrast inks. Card base contains title in `Bodoni Moda` with genre/metadata tags in `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Interactive Corner Notches:** Decorative red L-brackets at the top-left and bottom-right corners that animate outward on pointer hover.
- **Process Step Cards:** Minimalist tall frames topped with classical numeral typography (`01`, `02`) in large Bodoni serif, an illustrated icon badge, and a connecting directional vector running between steps.

### Chips, Badges & Labels
- **Editorial Tag:** Bracketed layout `[ PORTFOLIO ]` or `{ DETAIL PENGERJAAN }` rendered in `JetBrains Mono`, colored in `#8F1519`, centered with horizontal flanking hairline rules.
- **Status Pills:** Rectangular, sharp-edged chips with a 1px border and a miniature leading filled diamond `◆ Available for Commission`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Flat Washi paper background (`#FFFFFF` on `#FAF7F2`), crisp bottom-only or full 1px border (`#DDD5C8`), shifting to sharp `#8F1519` on focus. Monospaced input text with generous horizontal padding.
- **Submit / Action Groups:** Integrated input and button unit where the button feels like an ink stamp attached directly to the edge of the text field.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Navigation Bar:** Fixed parchment bar with a hairline separator. Navigation links are set in clean, uppercase tracking with a minimalist active indicator: double red dash (`--`) directly beneath the current route.
- **Editorial Quotation Blocks:** Left-bordered with a dual hairline (thick crimson + thin sumi), oversized Bodoni quotation marks (`“`), and digital artist signature stamp at the base.
